knoll/iron hive

an empty bench: the whole of lab 2's workshop — the camera, the dock, the wall, the tracing table and the sticker drawer — standing over bare paper, with nothing on it and nothing in the way. It is the room the Iron Hive kits were cut for. The bench moves like figma's canvas — hold the scroll wheel (or space) and drag to pan, scroll to pan, ctrl+scroll or pinch to zoom, shift 1 to fit what you have made on screen. Drag the bare paper and you pull a band out: everything it touches is picked, shift-click adds one or drops one, and dragging any of them carries the lot. Hold ctrl and the hitbox stops being the box: what answers the pointer is what is drawn under it, so you can take one tree out of a stand of them and the gaps between the branches belong to whatever is behind. The ? in the corner has the rest. The dock at the bottom draws on the paper itself; esc puts the pen down, and ctrl+z — or its undo button — takes back the last thing you moved or drew. Its sticker tool stands the sticker drawer up the left of the screen: rummage by kit or by name, pick one out, and click the paper to stamp it there, or drag one straight onto it. One that is already down can be pressed and held — or just dragged — to move it about without leaving the tool, and a quick tap on it still stamps another on top, which is how things get piled up. With the move tool up the band gathers stickers the way it gathers anything else: everything it touches is picked, and dragging any one of them carries the whole lot in one go, one ctrl+z to put back. Press a single one and it wears two handles — a corner at its bottom right to scale it by, and a ring on a stalk above it to turn it, shift for fifteen-degree steps. Double-click either handle to put that one back: the corner to the size the artwork was drawn at, the ring to upright. shift H and shift V mirror what is picked — one piece about its own middle, a group about the group's, so a row comes back reading the other way round; flip twice and you have exactly what you started with. Its upload tool puts the tracing table in the same place — lay a picture on it, trace it, file it, stamp it. Both panels are chrome, not scenery: they hold still while the bench moves under them. ctrl+c then ctrl+v copies whatever is picked and pastes it a nudge down-right — a group comes back as a group, still the same distance apart, and one ctrl+z takes the whole paste off again. Right-click a thing for its menu: raise and lower step it over or under the next thing it overlaps, and delete takes it off the paper (ctrl+z brings it back). Right-click one of a picked group and all three do the whole group at once, one ctrl+z between them.
