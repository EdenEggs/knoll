Standing is earned on the TOEM 2 wall, as one point for each day on which an edit of yours there goes live or is approved. Newcomers' edits wait for review; contributors' small edits go live at once; trusted editors' small and large edits go live at once. An edit to a piece that is not your own, or of a kind the wall's moderators have kept for themselves, waits for review. A drastic edit from a newcomer is refused; any other is decided by a vote of accounts with at least one point of standing, or by one of Knoll's moderators or the wall's maker.
