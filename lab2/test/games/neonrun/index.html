a game page, built by the Press Table and kept by hand from here on. It moves like lab 2: hold the scroll wheel (or space) and drag to pan, ctrl+scroll to zoom, shift 1 to fit the lot on screen, hold ctrl to pick by the drawing rather than the box; the ? in the corner has the rest. ctrl+s writes where everything stands back into this page's own index.html, through this folder's own door — never the bench's.

MOVING ABOUT
wheel drag
hold the scroll wheel down and drag — pans from anywhere
spacedrag
the hand: pans over anything, features included
drag
a sleeping feature moves; bare paper pulls a band
scroll
pan up and down — shift for sideways
ctrlscroll
zoom about the pointer (pinch does too)
+−
zoom in and out
shift0
back to 100%
shift1
fit every feature on screen
hv
hand tool, then back to the pointer
click
a feature to wake it up and use it
shiftclick
pick a feature, or drop it from the pick
ctrlhold
the hitbox becomes the drawing — one sticker out of a pile
ctrls
save this arrangement as the default — reset layout comes back here
ctrlz
undo — the last thing moved, or the last thing drawn
ctrlcv
copy what is picked, and paste a working copy of it
right click
a feature — raise it, lower it, or delete it (undo puts it back)
esc
let it go, put the pick down, drop the drawing tool
