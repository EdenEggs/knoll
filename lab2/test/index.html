knoll/test

the Press Table's sandbox: lab 2's runtime on a page of its own, with one caption and forty-eight kit parts — a stand of eight, five from the forest and three from the village, and the tray of forty stickers under it, the whole core sheet in Knoll's colours. It moves like lab 2: hold the scroll wheel (or space) and drag to pan, ctrl+scroll to zoom, shift 1 to fit the lot on screen, hold ctrl to pick by the drawing rather than the box; the ? in the corner has the rest. The autosave door is this folder's own server — node lab2/test/serve.js 4322 from site/ — and never the live bench's.

MOVING ABOUT
wheel drag
hold the scroll wheel down and drag — pans from anywhere
spacedrag
the hand: pans over anything, features included
drag
a sleeping feature moves; bare paper pulls a band
scroll
pan up and down — shift for sideways
ctrlscroll
zoom about the pointer (pinch does too)
+−
zoom in and out
shift0
back to 100%
shift1
fit every feature on screen
hv
hand tool, then back to the pointer
click
a feature to wake it up and use it
shiftclick
pick a feature, or drop it from the pick
ctrlhold
the hitbox becomes the drawing — one tree out of a wood
ctrls
save this arrangement as the default — reset layout comes back here
ctrlz
undo — the last thing moved, or the last thing drawn
ctrlcv
copy what is picked, and paste a working copy of it
right click
a feature — raise it, lower it, or delete it (undo puts it back)
esc
let it go, put the pick down, drop the drawing tool
