HEAR YE, HEAR YE
(or read ye, reading is fine)
A PROCLAMATION
issued from atop the Knoll
WHEREAS a page kept by one person is merely a page, and a page kept by everyone is a Knoll;
WHEREAS this hill has no landlord, only tenants with opinions;
WHEREAS a tome ye cannot doodle in is a sad tome indeed;
WHEREAS nobody asked the gnomes, and they showed up anyway;
BE IT HEREBY DECREED:
I.
Any soul may draw, paste, stamp, or scribble upon the hill. Skill is welcome but not required.
II.
Nothing is added to the hill by fiat. It is added by vote, and the vote is cast by you. Yes, you. Stop looking behind you.
III.
What the hill approves, the hill keeps. Forever. (Forever is subject to a later vote.)
IV.
What the hill declines is not destroyed. It is thanked for its service and shown gently to the compost.
V.
The hill is devoted to one subject at a time, chosen by the hill. Other subjects may petition for a hill of their own.
VI.
The gnomes are not staff. They simply live here. Please do not tip them; it makes them weird.
Signed in ink, mud, and one crayon,
THE ASSEMBLY OF THE KNOLL
